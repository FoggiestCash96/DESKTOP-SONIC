The second demo of **Sonic Velocity Run**, a free, open-source 3D Sonic fan game made in Godot: all eight acts of its first world, **Emerald Shore**, now with Sonic's full moveset, and fixes for what players ran into in v0.1.0. It's pre-alpha, so expect rough edges.

## What's new

**The rest of the moves:**

- **Stomp:** roll in the air (Ctrl / B) to slam straight down, breaking badniks and monitors on the way.
- **Drop dash:** with nothing to home in on, hold the second jump press, and keep holding it as you land to launch into a roll.
- **Super Peel Out:** standing still, hold drift (C / LT) and tap jump to rev, then let go of drift to take off running.
- **Drift:** hold drift while running fast and steering to slide round tight corners.
- **Light speed dash:** near a ring, press special (F / Y) to zip along a trail of rings, even across a gap.
- **Wall-jumps and wall-running**, on marked walls: jump into a wall-jump panel to cling and kick off it, or run fast and jump at a gold strip to run along the wall.

The test room on the main menu has a spot for each move. In Emerald Shore, there's a wall-jump climb in Act 2, a wall to run along in Act 5 and a ring trail for the light speed dash in Act 7.

**Fixes, from playing v0.1.0:**

- **The homing attack locks on to springs**, and launches you straight along them, ready to home in on whatever's next.
- **Going in and out of the 2.5D side views is forgiving.**
  - Coming in off-centre or round a corner, you're eased onto the path instead of running off its edge.
  - Coming out, the camera swings round behind you and the controls keep you going the way you were going, so you don't veer off the track.
  - Turning into Act 5's great hall no longer drops you into its pits, or out of the level through a gap at the tunnel's mouth.

## Playing

- **Windows:** unzip `SonicVelocityRun-windows.zip` and run `SonicVelocityRun.exe`. If Windows warns about an unrecognized app, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** extract `SonicVelocityRun-linux.tar.gz` and run `SonicVelocityRun.x86_64`.
- **macOS:** unzip `SonicVelocityRun-macos.zip`. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it, right-click it and choose **Open**.

You need a graphics card that supports Vulkan (Metal on macOS). Press Enter (or Start) on the title screen, then **Play**. Saves from v0.1.0 carry over.

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Left stick |
| Jump (hold for a higher jump) | Space | A |
| In the air: homing attack or air dash | Space again | A again |
| In the air: drop dash | Hold Space again, and land | Hold A again, and land |
| Roll (while moving); stomp (in the air) | Ctrl | B |
| Spin dash: hold still, tap jump to charge, let go | Ctrl, then Space | B, then A |
| Drift (running fast, steering) | Hold C | Hold LT |
| Super Peel Out: hold still, hold drift, tap jump to rev, let go of drift | C, then Space | LT, then A |
| Light speed dash (near rings) | F | Y |
| Boost (in the air, an air boost) | Shift | X or RT |
| Quickstep, or hop to the next rail | Q / E | LB / RB |
| Camera | Mouse or arrow keys | Right stick |
| Pause | Esc | Start |
