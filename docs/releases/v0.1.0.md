The first public demo of **Sonic Velocity Run**, a free, open-source 3D Sonic fan game made in Godot: all eight acts of its first world, **Emerald Shore**. It's pre-alpha, so expect rough edges.

## What's in it

- **Eight acts:** Sunny Beachfront, Lush Palm Grove, Cascading Waterfall Basin, Shallow Reef Lagoon, Underground Grotto, Tidal Cavern, Sunset Ridge Cliffs, and Stormy Coastal Fortress, where the Egg Tidecrab rises out of a tidal wave.
- **Moves:** momentum physics with slopes and loops, rolling and the spin dash, the homing attack and air dash, the boost and quickstep, grinding on rails, and swimming (with drowning, the classic way).
- **Stages:** springs, dash panels, item monitors, checkpoints, tidal waves to ride, currents and tides, moving and crumbling platforms, walls to smash, 2.5D side-view stretches, and four kinds of badnik. Every act hides five red rings, and Acts 1 to 7 an extra life.
- **Menus:** ten save slots, a world map, options for video, audio, the camera and the controls (all remappable), and accessibility options: a slower game speed, an invincibility assist and a bigger HUD.
- **Art and sound:** toon-shaded art, original music for every act, and about 50 sound effects, all made by scripts in this repository.

## Playing

- **Windows:** unzip `SonicVelocityRun-windows.zip` and run `SonicVelocityRun.exe`. If Windows warns about an unrecognized app, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux:** extract `SonicVelocityRun-linux.tar.gz` and run `SonicVelocityRun.x86_64`.
- **macOS:** unzip `SonicVelocityRun-macos.zip`. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it, right-click it and choose **Open**.

You need a graphics card that supports Vulkan (Metal on macOS). Press Enter (or Start) on the title screen, then **Play**.

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move | W A S D | Left stick |
| Jump (hold for a higher jump) | Space | A |
| In the air: homing attack or air dash | Space again | A again |
| Roll (while moving) | Ctrl | B |
| Spin dash: hold still, tap jump to charge, let go | Ctrl, then Space | B, then A |
| Boost (in the air, an air boost) | Shift | X or RT |
| Quickstep, or hop to the next rail | Q / E | LB / RB |
| Camera | Mouse or arrow keys | Right stick |
| Pause | Esc | Start |

## Known issues
