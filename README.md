# Sonic Velocity Run

This is my open source game. Enjoy!

**Sonic Velocity Run** is a free, open-source 3D Sonic fan game made in [Godot](https://godotengine.org/). It aims for momentum-first movement with classic slope physics, boost-era moves, and Adventure-style stages spread across six worlds.

> **Status: pre-alpha.** All eight acts of the first world, **Emerald Shore**, are playable, with their art and music: from **Act 1, Sunny Beachfront**, a speed act with loops, tidal waves to ride up the beach and a side-view pier, through a jungle's treetops, waterslides down a waterfall basin, a reef lagoon full of badniks, an underground grotto, a cavern that floods with the tide and a cliff-top run at sunset, to **Act 8, Stormy Coastal Fortress**, a short run to the world's first boss, a crab mech that rises out of a tidal wave. The core physics, the camera and the gimmicks are in: slopes and loops, rolling and the spin dash, the homing attack and air dash, the boost (with an air boost) and quickstep, the drop dash, Super Peel Out, stomp, drift and light speed dash, wall-jumps and wall-running on marked walls, grind rails, swimming and drowning, rings and losing them, springs, dash panels, item monitors, checkpoints, currents and tides, moving and crumbling platforms, breakable walls, five hidden red rings per act, and four kinds of badnik. There are ten save slots, a world map to pick acts from, an options menu (video, audio, camera, remappable controls), a pause menu, and accessibility options: a slower game speed, an invincibility assist, and a bigger HUD. A greybox test room for trying everything is on the main menu. All the art is toon-shaded and built by scripts in Blender: Emerald Shore's banded sandstone coast with its palms, jungle, waterfalls, reefs and caves, Act 8's fortress in a storm, the springs, monitors and other objects, the badniks, the boss, the title screen's beach, and Sonic himself, with Sonic Colors-style speed lines and motion blur. The music and sound are in: original tracks for the menus, all eight acts, the boss and invincibility, with jingles and about 50 sound effects, all made in code.

## Download

Windows, Linux and macOS builds are on the [Releases page](https://github.com/FoggiestCash96/DESKTOP-SONIC/releases). The first demo, **v0.1.0**, has all eight acts of Emerald Shore.

## What's planned

- **Six worlds** with 8 acts each, ending in a boss: Emerald Shore, Toxic Citadel, Starlight Strip, Glacier Peak, Magma Mine and Cloudtop Citadel. Difficulty rises world by world, and the last boss is the hardest.
- **Moves:** boost, homing attack, air dash, drift, quickstep, spin dash, drop dash, Super Peel Out, stomp, grind rails, wall-running and swimming.
- **Hybrid stages:** full 3D running mixed with 2.5D side-view path sections.
- **Super Sonic**, unlocked after the final boss.
- A world map, a lives system, and saves for best times.

The full design is in [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).

## Build and run from source

See [DEVELOPER.md](DEVELOPER.md). In short: install Godot 4.7.2 and Git LFS, clone the repo, open `project.godot` in Godot, and press F5.
